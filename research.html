In progress · Dissertation

Lights That Save Lives: Energy Access and Maternal Mortality in Sub-Saharan Africa

I estimate the effect of household electricity access and reliability on maternal health, using Demographic and Health Surveys from ten countries (Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Zambia, Malawi, Ethiopia, and Mozambique) from 2012 onward. Distance to the grid serves as an instrument, and nighttime lights from VIIRS satellites help measure where power actually flows.

Working papers

2026 · Working Paper

Household Energy Access, Infant Mortality and Facility Delivery in Nigeria

A study of the 2003 Nigeria DHS that asks whether infants in homes with electricity were more likely to survive, and whether their mothers were more likely to give birth in a health facility.

2026 · Working Paper

Spatial Econometric Evidence on Agribusiness Expansion and Environmental Trade-Offs in the U.S. Midwest

Uses spatial econometric models with county-level data to measure how agribusiness expansion relates to greenhouse gas emissions, both within a county and across its borders.

2026 · Working Paper

Energy Prices, Public Investment and Growth: Modeling Nigeria's Petrol Subsidy Removal

Builds a Ramsey–Cass–Koopmans growth model with an energy input, calibrated to Nigerian data, to trace what the 2023 subsidy removal means for consumption, capital, and growth, and how far reinvesting the savings can offset the shock.
