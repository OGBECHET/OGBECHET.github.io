PhD Candidate · Southern Illinois University Carbondale

Theresa Ogbeche

Applied Economist: Energy, Environment & Development

Carbondale, Illinois

I study what happens to people's health and livelihoods when the power doesn't come on. My work looks at energy access and electricity reliability in Sub-Saharan Africa, and how they shape maternal and infant health, along with the environmental costs of growth here in the United States. I use econometrics, household survey data, and satellite data to get at these questions.

Areas of Inquiry

01

Energy Access and Maternal Health

Does reliable electricity save mothers' and babies' lives? I connect DHS survey data, health facility data, and nighttime lights across ten Sub-Saharan African countries to find out.

View project →
02

Energy Prices, Policy and Growth

Macroeconomic models of how energy price shocks and public investment play out in a real economy, starting with Nigeria's 2023 petrol subsidy removal.

View project →
03

Agriculture, Emissions and Space

Spatial econometric work on how agribusiness expansion across the U.S. Midwest trades off against greenhouse gas emissions.

Corporate Decarbonization
